HIPAA Privacy Guidelines - Mindbowser Demo Clinic

Breach response: Suspected unauthorized access must be reported to the Privacy Officer within 24 hours.

Protected Health Information (PHI) includes names, medical records, billing details, and any data that can identify a patient.

Staff may share PHI only with the care team and authorized individuals listed on the patient's release form. Never discuss PHI in public areas.

Patients may request a copy of their medical records. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days. Identity verification is required.

Electronic access: Patient portal accounts must use unique passwords. Staff must lock screens when leaving a workstation.

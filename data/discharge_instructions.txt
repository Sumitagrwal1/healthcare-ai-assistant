Patient Discharge Instructions - Mindbowser Demo Clinic

After discharge, rest at home for at least 48 hours. Take all prescribed medicines exactly as written on the discharge summary.

Wound care: Keep the surgical site clean and dry. Change dressings once daily. Contact the clinic if you notice fever above 100.4 F, increasing redness, or pus.

Follow-up: Schedule a follow-up visit within 7 days of discharge. Bring your discharge papers and current medication list.

Diet: Drink plenty of fluids. Avoid alcohol for 72 hours if pain medication was prescribed. Eat light meals for the first day.

Emergency: Call emergency services for chest pain, sudden shortness of breath, or uncontrolled bleeding. The nurse advice line is available 24 hours at the number printed on your discharge sheet.
